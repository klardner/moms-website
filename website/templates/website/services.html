SERVICES I OFFER

General Counselling and Psychotherapy

I provide a safe space for clients to explore what's going on for them and help them to develop a more compassionate understanding of themselves and their life events. Counselling and psychotherapy involve the provision of professional assistance to people who are experiencing personal issues, in order to help alleviate those difficulties. Counselling tends to deal more with immediate issues that may have arisen more recently e.g. bereavement or relationship breakdown. Psychotherapy tends to deal with deeper, more longterm issues that may be rooted in the past e.g. a trauma, or serious mistreatment where the effects of such are ongoing.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) is a talking therapy that can help you manage your problems by changing the way you think and behave. It is most commonly used to treat anxiety and depression, but can be useful for other mental and physical health problems. Using CBT techniques I will help you "re-frame" your reactions and provide you with valuable tools to cope with life's obstacles.

Bereavement and Grief Counselling

Bereavement counselling involves supporting individuals who have experienced the loss of a loved one. This kind of support helps them work through their grief with its accompanying emotions and stages, as well as perhaps learning coping mechanisms to help during this difficult time. Grief counselling is not only for people who have lost a loved one to death. Grief counselling can help with many forms of change and loss, as there is still a process of mourning involved.

Online Counselling

Online Counselling can be done at a time and place that suits you from the comfort of your own home. The same principles that govern the client-therapist relationship in face-to-face settings apply in online therapy, such as a safe non-judgmental space and confidentiality.
