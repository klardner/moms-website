SERVICES

Individual Counselling

One-to-one therapy sessions tailored to your individual needs. Sessions typically last 50 minutes and provide a safe, confidential space for you to explore your thoughts, feelings, and experiences.

Anxiety & Stress

Support for managing anxiety, panic attacks, and stress-related difficulties. Together we can explore the root causes and develop effective coping strategies.

Depression

Compassionate support for those experiencing depression, low mood, or feelings of hopelessness. Therapy can help you understand and work through these difficult feelings.

Bereavement & Loss

Sensitive support through the grieving process, whether through the death of a loved one, relationship breakdown, or other significant losses in life.

Trauma

Specialist support for those who have experienced traumatic events. Working at your pace, we can process and integrate these experiences in a safe therapeutic environment.

Relationship Issues

Exploring patterns in relationships, communication difficulties, boundary setting, and building healthier connections with others.

Self-Esteem & Confidence

Working together to build a stronger sense of self, develop self-compassion, and overcome negative self-beliefs that may be holding you back.

Life Transitions

Support during times of change such as career shifts, retirement, becoming a parent, or other major life events that can feel overwhelming.
